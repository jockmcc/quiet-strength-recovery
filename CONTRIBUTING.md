# Contributing to Quiet Strength Recovery

Thank you for helping make recovery support easier to access.

Please keep contributions:
- free of advertising and tracking
- privacy-first and local-first where practical
- non-clinical: do not present QSR as diagnosis, treatment, counselling, psychotherapy or detox management
- person-centred: users choose what helps and leave what does not
- accessible and understandable in plain language

Do not commit secrets, signing keys, personal recovery records, API keys or private credentials.

By contributing code to this repository, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's GPL-3.0-only licence.
