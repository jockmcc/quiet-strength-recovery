# Quiet Strength Recovery

Quiet Strength Recovery is a free, optional self-guided recovery guide based on lived experience.

It shares practical recovery tools, reflections, daily check-ins, recovery milestones, grounding tools, urges-and-cravings tools, and links to recognised support services.

## Important disclaimer
Quiet Strength Recovery is a peer-support and educational recovery guide created from lived experience. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition or mental health disorder. The information and tools provided are not a substitute for professional medical advice, clinical therapy or emergency medical intervention. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you or someone else is in immediate danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline.

## Privacy
The app does not require a Quiet Strength account. Personal workbook entries are designed to remain on the user's device and are encrypted locally. This build contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and no Quiet Strength cloud workbook sync.

## Installation note
Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager because this APK is distributed directly rather than through Google Play. Only install the APK from this official release page or the official Quiet Strength Recovery website.
