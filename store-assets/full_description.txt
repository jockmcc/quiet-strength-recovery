Quiet Strength Recovery is an educational recovery guide for adults aged 18 and over, built from lived experience.

I share the reflections, routines and practical tools that helped me. You choose what is useful to you. There is no requirement to complete every page.

Inside the guide:
• Daily check-ins and a private dated archive
• Personal recovery-day counter, milestone badges and printable certificates
• Reflection tools for urges, feelings and the next useful choice
• Grounding and the BRAVE pause-and-choice tool
• Support mapping, boundaries and accountability reflections
• Links to recognised support services

Your writing stays on your device, with passcode protection and local encryption. No Quiet Strength account, payment or subscription is required. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs and no cloud journal sync.

Core tools can reopen offline after an initial successful load. External support and video links need an internet connection. You can save an encrypted full backup; individual text and PDF exports are readable files that you should store privately.

Quiet Strength Recovery shares educational tools and signposts support. It does not monitor your writing, contact anyone for you or provide live crisis support.
